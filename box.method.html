Once upon a time, in a quiet forest, there was a small tree who always looked up at the tall ones around it. “I wish I could be as big and strong as them,” it whispered every morning. The tall trees laughed kindly, “Be patient, little one. Growth takes time.” Seasons passed—rain nourished its roots, sunshine warmed its leaves, and gentle winds taught it to bend without breaking. Slowly, the little tree grew taller, stronger, and greener. One day, a bird built its nest on the little tree’s branches. The tree realized it didn’t need to be the tallest to be important—it just needed to be itself. And from that day, the little tree stopped wishing and started smiling.
